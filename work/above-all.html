Above All

Branding

Above All is a conceptual world tour identity for Korean musician Liunicorn, inspired by the message of his music: “Above All Else, It Must Be Love.” The project translates themes of love, faith, and connection into a cohesive visual experience. Drawing inspiration from nature, the identity uses organic forms, colors, and atmospheric imagery to reflect the emotional qualities of his music. The visual system extends across concert posters, stage design, merchandise, and a promotional website, creating a unified experience that connects the artist, his music, and the audience.
