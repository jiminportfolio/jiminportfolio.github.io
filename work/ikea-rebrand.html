IKEA Rebrand

Branding, 2026

IKEA rebrand is a conceptual brand extension that reimagines IKEA's identity beyond furniture, introducing a fragrance collection designed to complete the atmosphere of a home. Inspired by IKEA's modular design philosophy, the project explores how scent can become part of everyday living. The visual identity combines IKEA's recognizable design language with a distinct new expression, using typography, color, and form to communicate different fragrances. The system extends across fragrance products, packaging, and catalogs, creating a cohesive sensory experience that connects furniture, space, and scent.
