Curva

Branding

Curva is a modular furniture brand built around the idea of flexibility, simplicity, and sustainability. Designed with recycled materials and a glue-free assembly system, Curva explores how furniture can adapt to changing spaces and needs. Its modular structure allows individual components to connect and transform, encouraging a more flexible approach to everyday living. The brand identity reflects these principles through a minimal yet playful visual language, emphasizing the relationship between form, function, and connection.
