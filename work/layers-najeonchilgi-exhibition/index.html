Layers

Branding, 2026

Layers is a visual identity for a cultural event celebrating Najeonchilgi, the traditional Korean craft of mother-of-pearl inlay. Inspired by the way pearls form through layers building up over time, the identity explores the ideas of accumulation, connection, and transformation. These concepts are translated into connected letters, overlapping elements, and organic shapes derived from the logo itself. The visual system extends across posters, tickets, and event materials, bringing a contemporary perspective to traditional craftsmanship while maintaining a cohesive identity.
